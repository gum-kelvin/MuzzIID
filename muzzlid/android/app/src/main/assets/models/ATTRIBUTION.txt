MuzzlID research embedding baseline

Training data: Beef Cattle Muzzle/Noseprint database for individual identification.
Creators: Yijie Xiong, Guoming Li, Galen Erickson (2022).
DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6324361
Source: https://zenodo.org/records/6324361

Changes: identities with fewer than seven images excluded; identity-level split;
ImageNet ResNet50 features and an ArcFace-trained 512-D projection exported to ONNX.

Architecture reference: https://github.com/darasiemi/Animal_Biometrics_System
No source code or pretrained weights were copied from that repository.
Backbone initialization: torchvision ResNet50 IMAGENET1K_V2.
Torchvision source: https://github.com/pytorch/vision (BSD 3-Clause).
Research prototype; identification results require field validation.

Changes for this application: fixed [1,3,384,384] ONNX export (opset 17),
INT8 per-channel dynamic quantization of MatMul/Gemm weights,
L2-normalized 1536-D outputs and local small-pilot threshold calibration.
Input preprocessing follows the user's model-card example: RGB bilinear
384x384 resize, values /255, mean [.5,.5,.5], std [.5,.5,.5].
No image or personal record is sent to Hugging Face by the application.
